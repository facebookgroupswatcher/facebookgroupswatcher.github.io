How Facebook Groups Watcher Works

  1. Subscribe to the RapidAPI Facebook Group Scraper API. This powers the underlying group data the extension reads from.
  2. Add your API key to the extension. A one-time setup step inside the extension's settings panel.
  3. Create monitor threads. Pair keywords with the specific Facebook group URLs you want tracked.
  4. Set your check interval. Choose anywhere from every 30 seconds up to 24+ hours per monitor.
  5. Get notified instantly. Receive a browser notification the moment a matching post is found.
  6. Review and export leads. Open the dashboard to see captured leads, or export everything as JSON for your CRM.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Facebook Groups Watcher work with any Facebook group?

You add the specific group URLs you want to monitor, so coverage depends on the groups you have access to and choose to track.

How fast are the notifications?

Matching posts trigger a browser notification within 30 seconds, depending on the check interval you set for that monitor thread.

Do I need a RapidAPI account?

Yes. The extension relies on the RapidAPI Facebook Groups Scraper API for data, so you'll need to subscribe and connect your API key during setup.

Are my keywords private?

Keyword matching is performed locally in your browser rather than on a remote server, keeping your search terms under your control.

Can I export the leads I capture?

Yes. All captured leads can be downloaded as JSON, making it straightforward to import them into your CRM or spreadsheet of choice.
